# Battery Calibration

Use this procedure when a Milwaukee battery (an M12 camera battery or M18 access point battery) won't charge because the charger can't read it.

## Symptoms

When you put the battery on the charger, the charger LED shows **red and green at the same time**. The charger can't read the battery's charge state, so it doesn't charge it.

## Fix

1. Take the battery off the charger and find the **small button on the front of the battery** (the fuel gauge button)
2. Press and hold the button until the battery's LEDs **flash several times**, then **turn off completely**
3. Put the battery back on the charger
4. The charger LED should now show **solid red**: the charger is reading the battery and charging it

:::tip Confirmed fix
If the charger LED shows solid red after you put the battery back, the calibration worked.
:::
