## Charging

A charger is not included. We recommend the **Milwaukee M12-18C3** rapid charge station: it holds three M12 and three M18 packs and charges three at a time. A 3.0Ah (12V) battery charges in about 48 minutes and a 5.0Ah (18V) battery in about 63 minutes. See [About VTS](/docs/about-vts#battery-charging).
